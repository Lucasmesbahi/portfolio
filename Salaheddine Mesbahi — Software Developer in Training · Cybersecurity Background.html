01 — About

From securing systems to building software.

I graduated in 2026 with a Bac+2-level qualification in Digital Infrastructure, specializing in Cybersecurity, from CMC Casablanca-Settat. That training taught me to look at systems the way an attacker might: where can this break, and what happens when it does? Since September 2026 I’ve been studying software development at YouCode, turning that mindset into code.

I learn best by building. I’m drawn to backend systems, secure application development, and small security tools that solve a real problem, and I’d rather ship a modest project and learn from it than only read about a topic. I’m looking to grow into secure, reliable software engineering, one practical project at a time.
